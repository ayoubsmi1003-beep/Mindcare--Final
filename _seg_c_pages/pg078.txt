2.16.6 Underlying cause of death (UCOD) 
The underlying cause of death (UCOD), as defined in Section 2.15.1, is the condition selected 
for single-cause tabulation of mortality statistics. 
A condition that is provisionally considered as the underlying cause of death when applying 
the instructions step by step is referred to as a ‘tentative underlying cause of death (TUC)’ 
and may change several times as the instructions are applied to the death certificate.
